# SEO Content Brief — invoice-templates-now.com

Draft for human review. Nothing here has been applied to live pages. All copy is grounded strictly in facts already present on the site: free, no registration, works offline, data stays on device (no cloud storage), 100+ currencies, PDF export, client/invoice-status management, Chrome extension.

## 1. Target keywords (prioritized)

Priority order: US → Tier-1 English (UK/CA/AU) → Indonesia/Philippines (English-only search behavior) → rest of world.

1. **free invoice generator** — high intent, matches "free" + "no registration" facts; strong across all tiers.
2. **invoice generator chrome extension** — exact match to product category; low competition, high relevance.
3. **invoice generator for freelancers** — matches client-management + currency facts; strong in US/UK/CA/AU freelancer markets.
4. **offline invoice generator** — differentiator vs. SaaS competitors (no internet connection required); relatively low competition.
5. **invoice generator no sign up** — captures privacy/no-registration angle, appeals broadly including PH/ID markets where account-free tools are favored.
6. **invoice maker chrome extension** — secondary phrasing variant of #2, captures "invoice maker" search variant.
7. **invoice template extension** — protects current brand/name equity while adding category context.
8. **create invoice PDF online free** — captures PDF export + free intent, broad international volume including ID/PH.

Current H1 is "Invoice Template" (brand name only, no category keyword). All options below insert "invoice generator" while staying literally true to existing page facts.

**Option A**

- Subhead: "Create professional invoices instantly, right in your browser — no registration, no cloud, no subscription. Works offline and exports straight to PDF."

**Option B**
- H1: "Invoice Generator — Free Chrome Extension"
- Subhead: "Built for freelancers and small businesses: 100+ currencies, client management, and PDF export, with all your data staying on your device."

**Option C**
- H1: "The Free, Offline Invoice Generator for Chrome"

Recommendation: Option A is closest to current tone/length and slots cleanly into the existing `.hero h1` styling (clamp 36–64px); B leans harder into the freelancer segment; C leads with the offline/privacy differentiator, which is less commonly claimed by competitors.

## 3. Additional content section ideas (grounded only in real facts)

1. **"Invoice Generator vs. Spreadsheet" comparison** — A short comparison table/section contrasting manual spreadsheet invoicing (manual formulas, no PDF styling, no client history, re-typing data each time) against the extension's actual features: one-click PDF export, saved client records, built-in currency selection, no templates to rebuild each time. Emphasize *speed and simplicity*, not unverified time-savings numbers — no invented stats like "saves X hours/month" unless that data is sourced.

2. **"Who it's for" / freelancer use-case section** — Short, concrete use-case blurbs grounded in existing feature set: a freelance designer billing international clients in different currencies, a consultant who needs offline access while traveling, a small agency tracking multiple clients and invoice statuses. Each blurb ties directly to an already-documented feature (100+ currencies, offline mode, client management) rather than new claims.

3. **"Privacy by design" section** — Expand on the existing "data stays on your device" fact into its own short section explaining *why* that matters (no account to breach, no cloud sync risk, works without sending invoice/client data over the network). This reinforces an existing FAQ answer with more depth for both users and AI answer engines (GEO), without adding any new technical claims beyond what's already stated in the FAQPage schema.
