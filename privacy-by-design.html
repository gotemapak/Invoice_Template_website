Privacy by Design: Your Invoice Data Stays on Your Device

Invoices contain client names, contact details, line items, and payment amounts — the kind of information you don't want sitting on someone else's server. Here's exactly how Invoice Template handles it, and why that's different from most invoicing tools.

Install from Chrome Web Store

Why this matters for invoicing specifically

An invoice is a financial document. It usually includes a client's name, business or home address, email, and exactly what they're being charged and for what. Many invoicing tools store that in a cloud database tied to an account, which means a breach, a policy change, or a provider shutdown can expose it. Invoice Template is built as a Chrome extension that keeps that data on your own machine instead — there's no account to create, no server-side database, and no sync step where client records travel over the network.

Where this makes the biggest difference

💼

Freelancers billing international clients

Invoices sent abroad often carry a client's full name, address, and payment terms. Keeping that on your own device — rather than a third-party invoicing platform's servers — means one less place that data can be exposed, across any of the 100+ currencies you bill in.

🧳

Consultants working from shared or public networks

Filling out a client invoice on hotel wifi or a coworking network is a reasonable moment to not want that data transmitted anywhere. Because Invoice Template works offline and never uploads your data, the network you're on doesn't matter.

🗂️

Agencies holding multiple clients' financial details

An agency's client list is itself sensitive — it shows who you work with and what you charge them. Built-in client management keeps those records on the device you control, instead of in a shared cloud account other tools require.

Privacy questions, answered directly

Does Invoice Template send my invoice or client data anywhere?

No. The extension does not upload invoice or client data to any server. It's saved using the browser's local storage, on your device.

Do I need an account to use it?

No. There's no sign-up, no password, and no account of any kind. Install the extension and start creating invoices.

What happens to my data if I lose internet access?

Nothing changes — Invoice Template works completely offline, since it was never depending on a connection to save or load your data in the first place.

Is this the same as a "local-first" app?

Functionally, yes: your invoices and client records are created, saved, and exported entirely on your device, with no cloud database involved.
