Invoice Generator vs. Spreadsheet Invoicing

Still billing clients from a spreadsheet? Here's a straight comparison between rebuilding invoices by hand and using an offline invoice generator built as a Chrome extension.

Install from Chrome Web Store

Two ways to bill a client

Most freelancers and small businesses start by invoicing out of a spreadsheet, because it's already open and it's free. That works fine for the first invoice. The friction shows up on the tenth one: re-copying the template, fixing broken formulas, reformatting for a new currency, and manually turning a sheet into something presentable to send as a PDF. Invoice Template is a Chrome extension built to remove exactly that repeated manual work, without asking for an account or sending your data anywhere.

Frequently asked questions

Is a spreadsheet good enough for invoicing?

It can work for the occasional invoice, but most of the manual work — re-typing data, fixing formatting, converting to PDF — repeats every single time. A purpose-built invoice generator removes that repetition.

Does Invoice Template require an account?

No. There's no registration required.

Will my invoice and client data be uploaded anywhere?

No. Invoice Template does not use cloud storage, so your invoices and client information never leave your computer.

Can I still export a PDF like I would from a spreadsheet?

Yes. You can export any invoice as a PDF file for easy sharing, printing, and professional presentation — in one step, without adjusting page layout.

Does it support the currencies I bill in?

Invoice Template supports 100+ currencies, making it suitable for international businesses and freelancers.
