Invoice Template vs. AliExpress/Amazon Invoice Extensions

Some Chrome invoice extensions only work on one marketplace's order pages — built to scrape AliExpress or Amazon Seller Central, not to invoice a client. Here's how that compares to a general-purpose invoice generator.

Install from Chrome Web Store

Two different kinds of "invoice extension"

Several popular Chrome extensions marketed as invoice generators are actually marketplace scrapers: their permissions and content scripts only activate on AliExpress order-detail pages or Amazon Seller Central, because that's the only place they can read order data from. Invoice Template is built the other way around — a standalone form you fill in yourself, so it isn't tied to any marketplace, any site, or any single source of order data.

Frequently asked questions

Can Invoice Template generate an invoice from an AliExpress or Amazon order?

Invoice Template doesn't scrape order data from any marketplace — you fill in client, item, and payment details yourself. That makes it slower for a single AliExpress reorder than a purpose-built AliExpress scraper, but it works identically for every client and every purchase, not just ones made on a specific marketplace.

Why do some invoice extensions only work on one website?

Extensions built to auto-fill an invoice from a marketplace order need permission to read that marketplace's pages, so their Chrome permissions and content scripts are scoped to that one site. That's a reasonable design for a single-marketplace tool, but it means the extension can't be used for anything outside that marketplace.

Does Invoice Template require signing in or creating an account?

No. There's no sign-in and no account of any kind.

How many currencies does Invoice Template support?

138 currencies, selectable on every invoice regardless of your client's location.
